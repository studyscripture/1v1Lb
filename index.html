Official Black Ops 2 Rules

1. Gametypes & Game Settings

Health & Damage (ALL Modes): 30% Health, Normal Regen, Hit Indicator On, Headshots Only: No.
General (ALL Modes): Scorestreaks Not Allowed, Pre-Match: 15s, Pre-Round: 5s, Auto Team Balance: Off, Team Change: Allowed, Dynamic Map Elements: Yes, 3rd Person Spectating & CODcasting: Allowed, Scorestreak Delay: Off.

  • Free For All
    Time Limit: Unlimited | Score Limit: 20 Points (*Spawntrapping on Nuketown has a score limit to 50 points).
    Minimap: Constant | Killcam: Disabled (Enabled if Nonspawntrapping).
  • Hardpoint
    Time Limit: 5 Minutes | Score Limit: Unlimited | Teams: 2
    Lifetime: 45s | Capture Time: None | Locations: Linear Order | Scoring: Constant
    Minimap: Constant/Normal (Challenge Option) | Killcam: Off (Enabled if Nonspawntrapping)
    Spawns: Respawn Delay: None, Force Respawn: Yes, Wave/Suicide/Team Kill Penalties: None
  • Domination
    Time Limit: 5 Minutes | Score Limit: Unlimited | Teams: 2
    Capture Time: 5s | Round Limit: 2 | Switch Sides: Every Round
    Minimap: Constant/Normal (Challenge Option) | Killcam: Off (Enabled if Nonspawntrapping)
    Spawns: Respawn Delay: None, Force Respawn: Yes, Wave/Suicide/Team Kill Penalties: None

2. Class Setup

  • Primary: Ballista or DSR50 (Fast Mag or Extended Mag)
  • Attachments: FMJ
  • Perk 1: Flak Jacket
  • Perk 2: Scavenger or Fast Hands
  • Perk 3: Extreme Conditioning or Dead Silence
  • Deathstreak: Not Allowed

3. Warnings & Illegal Actions

For each game, players can receive up to two warnings. The second warning qualifies as a disqualification. Whether a warning or DQ is called is solely up to the victim.

  • Incorrect rules, setup, or illegal class.
  • Spawning out and spawning back in / Killing yourself with ammo.
  • Using/throwing equipment that corrupts the game (e.g. throwing a knife into a spawn to prevent a player from spawning there).
  • NO RING AROUND THE ROSIE FOR MORE THAN 2 MINUTES.

4. Disqualifications (DQ)

Disqualifications must be called immediately after an illegal action has occurred or it will be invalid. You cannot call a DQ after a match has finished (unless the action couldn't be seen during the game).

  • Killing with an illegal setup, rules, or class.
  • Killing a player joining/participating in the match incorrectly.
  • Point Glitching (Can result in DQ or a free kill for the victim player depending on their call).
  • Throwing Knives.
  • Hardscoping, hardscope jumpshots, relapse shots.
  • Killing a player with explosive objects (barrels, cars) is legal ONLY if the explosion was caused by a sniper shot.
  • Any form of cheating (Hacking/Mods, IP Flooding/DDoS, manipulating in-game mechanics/glitching, counting bullets or assistance from a non-competing player).

5. Settings & Disconnections

  • Host Responsibilities: The host must ensure correct rules. If settings are wrong, the host must end the game immediately before any action occurs, or it results in a DQ for that specific game. If a player notices settings are wrong but continues playing without notifying the host, it continues as a normal game. Refusal to end a wrongly-setup match = DQ.
  • Disconnections: Players have 5 minutes to rejoin and contact the referee/opponent, or they forfeit that game. The game resumes with the exact scores from the disconnect (Except in objective modes, where disconnecting results in an automatic loss for that game).

6. Reporting & Attendance

  • Scores must be reported within 24 hours. If one player fails to report or dispute, the reporting player automatically receives the win.
  • Reporting fake wins/losses without a match occurring results in suspension.
  • If a player fails to arrive, the other player must provide valid proof of absence to claim a forfeit win.
  • Players MUST use the exact PSN ID displayed on their 1v1LB Profile.
  • One account per player. Alt accounts, or playing yourself to gain points, result in a ban. Repeat matches between the same two players earn reduced Elo, and are unrated after the fourth in a week.

7. Disputes

  • If no proof or details are provided in a dispute, the win goes to the player who provided proof.
  • If no player submits proof or details within 5 hours of the dispute, a staff member will delete the match (no win/loss awarded).
  • Valid Proof: Clear pictures of the final scoreboard with players visibly present, video proof of the game, or observable evidence of an in-game action. Witnesses/messages do NOT count as proof.
  • Reporting false disputes or fake proof results in an immediate suspension from 1v1LB.
